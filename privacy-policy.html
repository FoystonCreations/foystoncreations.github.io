Privacy Policy

Foyston Creations · Last updated 19 November 2026

Foyston Creations is a small UK business that sells digital spreadsheet products for bakers through its Etsy shop. This policy explains what information we handle, including through the publishing tool we use to share our own products on Pinterest.

This website

This site only hosts a few simple pages used to run our shop. It has no accounts, forms, advertising or analytics, and it sets no cookies. We do not collect any information from visitors.

Our Pinterest publishing tool

We use a private tool that connects to our own Pinterest business account. It is used only by the shop owner, to create boards and pins that link to our own listings on Etsy.

  • It acts only on our own Pinterest account. It does not access anyone else's account, and it does not read, collect or store information about other Pinterest users.
  • The access token that Pinterest provides is stored privately on the owner's computer. It is never published or shared.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.
  • We can revoke the tool's access at any time from our Pinterest account settings.

Etsy customers

Purchases are made on Etsy, and Etsy processes your order, payment and personal details under its own privacy policy. We only see what Etsy shows to shop owners, and we use it only to fulfil your order and give support.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, or want us to delete information we hold about you, contact us at stevefoyston@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
